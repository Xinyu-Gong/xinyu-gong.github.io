About Me

Hi! I am Xinyu Gong, a Ph.D. student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, conducting my research at the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my B.S. degree in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2024.

My research focuses on multimodal image matching and registration, as well as remote sensing image processing. If you are interested in academic collaboration, please feel free to contact me.
